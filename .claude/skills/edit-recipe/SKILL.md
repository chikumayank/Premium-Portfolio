# Edit Recipe

The aim is an edit with its own personality. A recipe combines **pace, transition family, sound
design, caption style, grade and one signature move**. No combination should repeat within 4 posts.

## Inputs
Script or footage description, the editing app (from `creator/brand-profile.md`), the last 4 rows of
`creator/content-log.md`, and `creator/libraries.md` § Pacing, § Transitions, § Music, § Retention devices.

## Process
1. **Choose the recipe** and show it as a card:
   ```
   RECIPE NAME: "Monsoon Diary" (give it a memorable name)
   Pace: P04 breathing → P01 hyper on the reveal
   Transitions: T05 J/L-cuts + T13 sound-bridge (no flashy transitions)
   Music: M09 piano → natural rain sound only at climax
   Captions: hand off to type-and-color (suggest C08)
   Grade: hand off to type-and-color (suggest G04)
   Signature move: freeze-frame with handwritten note at the emotional beat
   ```
   Each element must differ from the last 3–4 posts unless the post is part of a marked series.
2. **Pacing map:** a timeline table showing where it is fast, where it breathes, and where the drop or reveal is.
   Give average shot lengths per section.
3. **Cut list:** beat by beat, showing the clip, in/out idea, transition, zoom/scale moves (e.g. 100→115% on emphasis),
   speed ramps, and on-screen element.
4. **Sound design pass** (this is what makes edits feel premium):
   - Music in/out points, ducking under voice (−12 to −18 dB), a music cut or stop on the punchline
   - SFX: whooshes on movement, risers before reveals, a pop or click on text appearing,
     ambience bed, and silence used deliberately
   - Voice: noise reduction, EQ (cut mud ~250Hz, presence ~3–5kHz), compression, loudness about −14 LUFS
5. **Effects budget:** at most 2 effect types per video. Pick them to serve the story.
6. **App how-to:** exact steps in the user's app for the 3 hardest moves (e.g. CapCut: keyframe
   zoom, auto-captions → edit style, speed curve; DaVinci: speed ramp with retime curve; Premiere:
   morph cut, essential sound).
7. **Export:** Reels/Shorts 1080×1920, 30fps (or 24/25 if shot so), high bitrate, captions kept within the
   safe zone (avoid the bottom ~20% and right ~15% where UI buttons sit). For long-form, 4K upload even if
   shot in 1080p, which gives better compression.

## Anti-monotony toolbox (rotate through these)
- Change the **aspect ratio** inside the video (letterbox section, 4:3 flashback).
- **Mixed media:** photo stills, screen recordings, scanned paper, stop-motion.
- **Text as character:** text that reacts, gets crossed out or argues with the VO.
- **Sound-only moments:** black screen with audio for 1s.
- **Speed contrast:** slow-mo detail against real-time chaos.
- **Re-framing:** punch-in crop of the same take to fake a second camera.
- **Breaking the 4th wall:** an edit note appears ("I cut this part because…").
- **Analog texture:** film burns, scan lines, paper overlays. Use these sparingly.

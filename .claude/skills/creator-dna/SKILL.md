# Creator DNA

Every other skill reads the profile this skill writes. It separates the **fixed** parts that make
you recognisable from the **variable** parts that keep each post fresh.

## Process

1. **Read** `creator/brand-profile.md` and `creator/content-log.md`. If the profile is filled in,
   offer to update only the parts that changed.
2. **Interview** in 3 short rounds of at most 5 questions each. Don't ask everything at once.
   - Round 1 (you): what you film now or want to film, why, platforms, language mix,
     hours per week, gear, editing app.
   - Round 2 (audience): who you picture watching, what they should feel or get, which
     creators they already watch, and what bores them.
   - Round 3 (taste): 3 creators, films or brands whose *look* you love and why; anything you
     hate seeing; your on-camera energy.
3. **Propose** 2–3 positioning options as a one-line statement plus a name for the angle, for example
   "Budget luxury traveller", "Honest engineer-life vlogger" or "Desi fitness without the bro". Recommend one.
4. **Define pillars** (3–5). For each: the viewer benefit, 3 example video titles, and a target share
   of posts. Include at least one personal or story pillar so the channel doesn't become all tips.
5. **Lock the fixed identity** (kept small on purpose):
   - 1 anchor font from `creator/libraries.md` that always appears (e.g. on the title card or name tag)
   - 2–3 core colours that appear somewhere in every post
   - 1 signature element: a catchphrase, sound, framing, object or outro move
6. **Open the variable zone:** choose which font pairings, grades, music moods and formats
   from `creator/libraries.md` fit the brand. Aim for at least 8 formats, 6 font pairings and 5 grades
   so later skills have room to rotate.
7. **Never-us list:** write down the overused things the creator refuses to do
   (e.g. "Montserrat yellow captions on everything", "trending audio unrelated to video",
   "'wait till the end'").
8. **Write** the result into `creator/brand-profile.md`, keeping its headings.
9. **Close** with a one-week starter plan: 5 posts, each a different format and pillar, as a table.

## Rules
- Be specific and opinionated. Avoid generic advice like "be authentic" or "post consistently".
- Respect the user's language mix (Hindi/Hinglish/regional is fine and often a strength).
- If they have no niche yet, suggest a 30-day **exploration sprint**: 3 pillars, 10 posts each, then
  keep the one with the best saves + shares per view.

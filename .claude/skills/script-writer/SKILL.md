# Script Writer

## Inputs
- The idea (and its hook from `hook-lab`, if there is one)
- `creator/brand-profile.md`: voice, language mix, phrases to keep or avoid
- `creator/content-log.md`: avoid story structures used in the last 3 posts
- `creator/libraries.md` § Story structures, § Retention devices

Ask for target length and platform if they aren't given. Defaults: 30–45s for Reels/Shorts, 8–12 min for vlogs.

## Short-form script format
Pick a structure (S-ID) and 2–3 retention devices (R-IDs). State them at the top. Then:

| Time | Beat | Spoken / VO | On-screen text | Visual / b-roll | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Hook | … | … | … | … |
| … | … | … | … | … | … |
| end | Loop/CTA | … | … | … | … |

Rules:
- Spoken pace is about 2.5 words/sec in English and about 3 words/sec in fast Hinglish. Check the word count against the time.
- Change something every 2–4 seconds: angle, text, b-roll or sound.
- One idea per video. Cut anything that doesn't serve the payoff.
- Make the ending loop into the start (R10) or give a specific CTA tied to the content
  (e.g. "comment ROUTE and I'll send the map"), not "like and subscribe".
- Write the way the creator talks: contractions, their slang, their language mix.

## Long-form vlog format
1. **Cold open (0:00–0:30):** the best moment, then a stakes line, then the title card
2. **Chapters:** 3–6 named chapters, each with a mini-hook, a turn and a payoff
3. **Re-hooks** every 60–90s ("but that's not even the crazy part…")
4. **Emotional beat:** one honest, quiet moment in the second half
5. **Ending:** a resolution plus a teaser for the next video. End without a long goodbye.

Include for each chapter: the talking points (not word-for-word unless asked), must-get shots, b-roll
ideas, and where the music changes.

## Output extras
- 3 title options + thumbnail text (≤ 4 words) for long-form
- Platform caption: a first line that works as a hook, 2–4 lines of value, a CTA and 3–5 specific hashtags
- Next step: "Run `shot-planner` for the shoot list."

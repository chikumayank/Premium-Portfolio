# News Explainer: "Headline → Wallet"

News reels lose value quickly. The goal is **accurate, angled and posted within 24–48 hours**.

## 1. Radar (find topics)
- Use web search (if available) for this week's India + world stories in: geopolitics, government policy,
  RBI/inflation/rupee, crude/gold, markets (index-level only), budget/GST/income-tax changes,
  NRI/FEMA/remittance rules, sports business (IPL, cricket deals, Olympics), big tech launches, and viral trends.
- If no search is available, ask the user to paste 3–5 headlines they've seen.
- Always check `creator/content-log.md` so the same story isn't covered twice unless there's a real update.

## 2. Pick (score 1–5 each)
| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Money angle | Can I say clearly how it affects the viewer's wallet, job, EMI, taxes or investments? |
| Curiosity | Is there a "why" people are confused about? |
| Shelf life | Will it still be relevant in 48 hours? |
| Safety | Can I explain it neutrally and with sources? |
| Fit | Does it suit P1–P4 in `creator/brand-profile.md`? |
Recommend the top 1–2 and say why.

## 3. Fact pack (before scripting)
- 3–5 **key facts**, each with a source. Prefer primary sources: RBI, SEBI, PIB/ministries, Income Tax Dept / CBDT,
  GSTN/CBIC, official team/league releases, company filings, then Reuters/PTI/major papers.
- Mark each fact ✅ verified (with link and date) or ⚠️ unverified (keep it out of the script or say "reports say").
- Note any number that may change (prices, rates). The script should say "as of <date>".
- Never invent figures. If unsure, leave it out.

## 4. Script (30–60s), default structure
1. **Hook (0–2s):** the headline as a question or contrast (H21–H24 in `creator/libraries.md`)
2. **What happened (≤ 10s):** 2 facts, no jargon
3. **Why it happened (≤ 15s):** one analogy (e.g. "Crude is like the country's petrol bill…")
4. **₹ ANGLE (≤ 15s):** show the "₹ ANGLE" tag. What it means for the viewer's money: 2–3 concrete effects
5. **What to do / watch (≤ 8s):** an educational takeaway, never a buy/sell call
6. **Close:** the signature line plus a CTA ("Comment your city, I'll check fuel prices there")
Then hand off to `edit-recipe` and `type-and-color`, using a format from F29–F34.

## 5. Compliance & safety checks (always print this checklist)
- [ ] **No investment advice.** Explain, don't recommend. No specific stock/crypto buy/sell/target price and no promised returns.
      Unregistered creators should stay educational, because SEBI has tightened rules around unregistered "finfluencers".
      (This is not legal advice. Check current SEBI rules if you mention markets often.)
- [ ] **If the creator is an ICAI member:** ICAI rules restrict CAs from soliciting clients or advertising their services.
      Educational content is generally fine, but no "DM me to file your ITR" and no claims of being better than other CAs.
      Check the latest ICAI guidelines on social media.
- [ ] **Employed at a firm (Big Four etc.):** no comments on specific listed companies (possible audit clients), nothing from work,
      personal account only, "Views are personal". See *Professional guardrails* in `creator/brand-profile.md`.
- [ ] **Politics/geopolitics:** neutral wording, both sides' stated positions, no party endorsement, no unverified claims about
      named people (defamation risk). Use "according to <source>" phrasing.
- [ ] **Reach note:** Instagram doesn't recommend political content to non-followers by default. Framing a political
      story through its **economic/money angle** is better for reach and fits the brand.
- [ ] Add a pinned comment or caption line with sources: "Sources: RBI press release (date), PIB (date)".
- [ ] Add a "Not financial advice. For education only." line in the caption for finance topics.

## 6. Fast-turnaround workflow (target: 90 minutes from idea to posted)
15 min radar + pick → 20 min fact pack → 15 min script → 15 min record → 25 min edit in Edits.
Keep 2 **evergreen backup reels** filmed in advance for weeks when the news is slow.

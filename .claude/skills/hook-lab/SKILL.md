# Hook Lab

A hook is **four layers firing together in the first 1–3 seconds**:
1. **Visual:** what's in frame 1 (motion beats stillness; a face or hands beats scenery)
2. **Text:** on-screen words (≤ 7 words, readable in 1 second)
3. **Spoken:** the first line (≤ 12 words, no "Hey guys, welcome back")
4. **Sound:** an SFX, a music hit or a natural sound that signals something is happening

## Process
1. Read the idea, `creator/brand-profile.md` (voice, never-us) and the last 5 rows of `creator/content-log.md`.
2. Choose **6 different archetypes** from `creator/libraries.md` § Hook archetypes, leaving out any used in
   the last 5 posts.
3. For each, write all four layers. Example:

   | Archetype | Visual (frame 1) | On-screen text | Spoken line | Sound |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | H03 Result-first | Finished room, slow push-in | "₹8k room makeover" | "This used to be the worst room in my house." | Whoosh into soft piano |

4. Rate each one for **clarity** (do I know what this video is?), **tension** (do I need to keep watching?)
   and **brand fit**. Recommend a top pick and a backup for A/B testing (Instagram trial reels or
   YouTube's test-and-compare titles/thumbnails for long-form).
5. For long-form vlogs, also give a **cold open (first 30s) plan**: a teaser of the best moment, a
   promise, then the title card with the anchor font.

## Banned openers (unless used ironically)
"Hey guys welcome back" · "In today's video" · "Wait till the end" · "Don't skip" ·
a logo intro longer than 1 second · a 3-second static establishing shot.

## Craft tips to apply
- Start the video *after* the first line has begun, or mid-movement.
- Put the payoff visual in the first frame and hide the explanation.
- Contrast is a strong hook device: big vs small, cheap vs costly, expected vs actual.
- Match the hook's promise to the video's payoff. Clickbait kills retention after second 5.

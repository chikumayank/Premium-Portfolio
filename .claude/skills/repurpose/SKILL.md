# Repurpose

Repurposing doesn't mean posting the same clip everywhere. Each piece gets **its own hook, format
and edit recipe**, chosen to suit the platform.

## Inputs
A transcript, timestamps, chapter list or description of the long video. Ask for a transcript if
they have one (YouTube auto-captions work). Also read `creator/brand-profile.md` and `creator/content-log.md`.

## Process
1. **Moment mining:** list 8–15 candidate moments with timestamps, tagged as:
   funny · insight · emotional · visual wow · controversial · how-to · behind-the-scenes.
2. **Pick 4–6** and give each one a **different** treatment:

| # | Source timestamp | Platform | New format (F-ID) | New hook (H-ID + line) | Edit recipe sketch | Length | Cover text |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

   Treatments to rotate: straight clip with a new hook, re-voiced recap, text-only scroller (F17),
   reaction to your own footage (F07), photo-dump (F24), "the part I almost cut", and a carousel of stills
   plus lessons.
3. **Platform tuning:**
   - **Instagram Reels:** emotion and shareability, cover readable on the grid, caption with a hook first line.
     Use trial reels to test.
   - **YouTube Shorts:** a stronger first-second hook, loop-friendly endings, link to the full video
     (related video feature).
   - **Carousel (IG):** 6–10 slides, slide 1 is the hook, the last slide saves or shares.
   - **Stories:** a poll or question sticker pulled from the video's topic.
   - **YouTube community post:** a poll plus a still.
4. **Posting schedule:** spread pieces across 7–10 days, alternating formats, with no two consecutive posts
   from the same source moment.
5. Write captions for each piece in the creator's voice.

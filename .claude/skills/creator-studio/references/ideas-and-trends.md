## 1. Ten idea sources (use at least 4 in each batch, and label each idea)
1. **Pillar × format matrix:** cross an under-used pillar with an under-used format (`libraries.md` §1)
2. **Audience pain this week:** deadlines, festivals, exams, salary day, tax season, travel season, weddings, monsoon
3. **Personal life mining:** something that actually happened to the creator this week. Ask them
4. **News hook:** what is in the headlines → the lens angle (see `news-finance-compliance.md`)
5. **Comment and DM mining:** every question is a video; every argument is a video
6. **Contrarian take:** a common belief in the niche that the creator disagrees with (with evidence)
7. **Series seed:** Part 1/2/3, Day 1–30, "Every Friday: ___"
8. **Search demand:** autocomplete in YouTube and Instagram search ("NRI tax…", "how to…")
9. **Remix a winner:** the creator's best post redone in a different format
10. **Trend mechanic:** take a trend's *structure* (a transition, a sound idea, a meme template), not its content

## 3. What creators are doing (late 2026 snapshot)
These are trend directions from industry coverage. They change monthly, so always check in-app (Reels tab, Edits "Inspiration"/ideas, YouTube Shorts feed) before committing.
- **Less polish, more personality:** selfie "yap" videos (unscripted, minimal edit, subtitles plus a text headline). Audiences report wanting less polished content.
  → Mix 1 raw video for every 2 produced ones.
- **Fast, hook-first edits:** 8–15 cuts per 30s in high-energy formats, captions timed to beats or pauses.
- **Longer short-form works:** 60–90s videos with strong value density often beat 15s clips. Shorts can run up to 3 min; Reels allow longer
  videos too (check current limits in-app). Length must be earned by the story, not padding.
- **Episodic series:** recurring named formats ("₹ Angle Monday", "NRI Desk #12") build return viewers.
- **Storytime to camera** with cutaways; **game-style or quiz formats** ("Guess the price", "Real or fake headline?").
- **Explainers with visual evidence:** green screen with articles or charts, highlighted documents, map zooms, paper or scrapbook textures.
- **Platform split:** Reels reward trend participation, visual polish and *sends*; Shorts reward value density, watch time and searchability.
- **AI-assisted editing is now standard:** auto-captions with word-level editing, AI cutouts, auto-reframing and auto-clipping of long videos.
  Use AI for speed, never for fake visuals in news or finance content.
- **Text-behind-subject, 3D-tracked text, subject outlines and glow** remain popular "premium" signals (`effects-library.md` §VFX).
- **Comment-reply videos and stitches** create community and series ideas.
- **Interactive layers:** polls in stories, "comment KEYWORD" for a resource (keep it educational; no lead-gen for regulated professionals).

## 4. Trend adaptation checklist
- [ ] Does the trend's *mechanic* fit the lens? (If you'd need to explain why you're doing it, skip it)
- [ ] Does it put the takeaway on screen within 3 seconds?
- [ ] Is the trending audio low in the mix, with the creator's own voice or content leading?
- [ ] Is it posted within the trend window (usually 3–10 days for audio or meme trends)?
- [ ] Is it on the never-us list? If so, skip it.

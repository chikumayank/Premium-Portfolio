# Strategy & Niche (ONBOARD)

## 1. The interview (3 rounds, at most 5 questions each)
- **You:** topics you could talk about for a year, your profession or credibility, languages, gear, editing app, hours per week, what you've posted before, current followers.
- **Audience:** who exactly (age, city or country, life stage), what they want (learn / understand / feel seen / escape / decide), and which creators they already watch.
- **Taste:** 3 creators, films or brands whose *look* you love; what you hate seeing; your on-camera energy (1–10); things you'll never do.

## 2. Positioning: "the lens" method
Creators with many interests should **not** run separate topic channels. Choose a **lens** that turns every topic into your content:
- Lens = *your credibility* × *what the audience cares about*.
  Examples: "money angle of every headline" (a CA), "science behind fitness myths" (a doctor), "budget-luxury travel" (a planner), "engineer explains tech launches".
- Write the positioning as: **"I help ___ understand/do ___ through ___."** Then turn it into a 4–7 word tagline (Hinglish is fine).
- Offer 2–3 options with trade-offs (reach vs depth vs ease) and recommend one.

## 3. Content pillars (3–5)
| Pillar | Viewer benefit | Share of posts | 3 example titles |
Rules: at least 1 pillar is **trust-building** (expertise), 1 is **reach** (news or trends), and 1 is **human** (personal, behind the scenes, off-duty).
Keep the reach pillar at or below 40% so the account doesn't become a news aggregator.

## 4. Fixed identity (kept small)
- **Core palette:** 2–3 colours that appear in every post (a text box, a background, an outfit or a prop)
- **Anchor element:** one recurring graphic (tag, lower-third, logo bug) that appears briefly
- **Signature:** a catchphrase, opening ritual, closing line, sound or framing
- **Cover system:** one cover layout family (with varied colours and images) so the grid looks consistent

## 5. Variable zone
List the allowed formats (8+), font styles (5+), grades (5+), music moods (5+) and caption styles (5+). This is the rotation pool.

## 6. Never-us list
Examples: ragebait thumbnails, "wait till the end", unrelated trending audio, the same yellow captions every time, stock tips, comedy skits (if off-brand).

## 7. Account setup and relaunch
- **Handle:** short, speakable, no numbers or underscores if possible. The name field should contain keywords (e.g. "Name | CA · Tax · NRI"), because the name field is searchable on Instagram.
- **Bio formula:** who you are + what you give + for whom + (language) + a personal touch. Add "Views personal" if employed.
- **Profile photo:** close crop of your face, plain contrasting background.
- **Highlights:** 3–4 matching your pillars, with covers in the brand palette.
- **Relaunch of a dormant account:** archive off-theme posts (don't delete them), post 3 on-theme reels in week 1 and pin the best 3.
  A dormant account isn't "penalised"; the bigger issue is that new visitors see an unclear theme.
- **Use a Professional (Creator) account** to get Insights and trial reels. Link YouTube and use the same handle where possible.

## 8. 30-day exploration sprint (if the niche is unclear)
3 pillars × 4 posts each. Judge only on **shares per 1k views, saves per 1k views and follows per 1k views**, then double down on the winner.

## 9. Outputs
Write or refresh `creator/brand-profile.md`, then produce a starter week (3–5 posts, each a different format and pillar).

# Platforms & Publishing (PUBLISH)

> Platform rules change often. Treat specific limits as "last known" and check in-app when in doubt.

## 1. Instagram Reels
- **What drives distribution:** watch time (especially the first 3s), **sends/shares per reach**, saves, likes, comments and follows from a reel. Sends are a key signal for reach to non-followers.
- **Originality:** original content is favoured; reposting others' content or videos with other apps' watermarks gets reduced reach. Always export clean.
- **Trial reels:** shown to non-followers first. Use them to test new formats or hooks without affecting your followers' feed.
- **Political content:** not proactively recommended to non-followers by default. Frame current affairs through an economic or personal-finance angle.
- **Hashtags:** use a few (3–5) specific ones. They help categorisation, not reach. Keywords in the caption and on-screen text matter more (Instagram search reads them).
- **Captions:** the first line is a second hook; 2–4 lines of value; a CTA; sources and disclaimers for finance.
- **Collabs:** the Collab feature (co-author) shares a reel to both audiences. This is great for growth with peers (other CAs, NRI creators, finance pages).
- **Stories:** the same day as the reel, use a poll or quiz sticker on the topic and share the reel to stories. Stories build relationships with existing followers.
- **Length:** 30–90s is the sweet spot for explainers. Longer reels are allowed, but the story must earn the length.

## 2. YouTube Shorts
- **Up to 3 minutes** (square or vertical). Value density and watch-through matter most; the loop count also helps.
- **Title:** ≤ 60 characters, keyword-first ("NRE vs NRO account explained | NRI tax"). Shorts are searchable.
- **Related video link:** link the Short to a related long video to drive subscribers to long-form.
- **Remove Instagram-only elements** (trending audio may not be licensed; swap it for a YouTube Audio Library track if needed).
- **Post timing:** a day after the reel is fine. Don't repost the same Short twice.

## 3. YouTube long-form (month 3+)
- 8–15 min explainers (for example a "₹ Angle of the month" deep dive, or an NRI tax guide).
- Titles ≤ 60 characters, a thumbnail with ≤ 4 words, chapters in the description, sources, and pinned-comment links.
- **Repurpose:** cut 4–6 Shorts from each long video (see `workflow-and-growth.md`).

## 4. Publish kit template
```
IG CAPTION
Line 1 (hook): …
Value (2–4 lines): …
CTA: Save / Send to ___ / Comment KEYWORD
Sources: …
Disclaimer: Educational only. Not financial/tax advice. Views are personal.
Hashtags (3–5): #…

YT SHORTS TITLE: …
YT DESCRIPTION: 1–2 lines + sources + disclaimer + #shorts (optional)

PINNED COMMENT: a question that sparks replies, or a source link
STORY: poll/quiz sticker "___?" + share the reel
TRIAL REEL? yes/no + what's being tested
```

## 5. When to post
Best times vary by account. Use Insights → "When your followers are active", then test 2 time slots for 2 weeks each.
India-focused accounts usually find early evening and late evening (around 7–10 pm IST) good. For NRI audiences, consider Gulf, UK or US evenings.
Consistency (3 posts a week every week) beats chasing the perfect hour.

## 6. Engagement (the first 60 minutes)
Reply to every comment with a full sentence or a question; pin the best comment; share to stories; send it to 2–3 relevant peers
(no engagement pods). Turn great comment questions into reply videos (format F18/F32).

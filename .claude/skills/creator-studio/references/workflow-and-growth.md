# Workflow, Repurposing, Analytics & Growth

## 1. Weekly system (5–7 hours → 3 reels)
| Day | Time | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | 60 min | IDEATE → pick 3 (different pillars and formats) → HOOK + SCRIPT for 2 evergreen reels |
| Mon | 60–75 min | Batch-shoot both evergreen reels (change top and set between them) + 10 b-roll clips |
| Mon/Tue | 60 min | Edit reel A → post Tue |
| Wed | 90 min | NEWS reel: radar → fact pack → script → record → edit → post Thu |
| Fri | 45 min | Edit reel B → post Sat |
| Sat | 30 min | Log all posts, AUDIT + REVIEW, plan stories |
| Daily | 5 min | Reply to comments, re-upload yesterday's reel as a YouTube Short |

## 2. Repurpose (one long video or shoot → many pieces)
1. **Moment mining:** 8–15 timestamped moments tagged funny · insight · emotional · visual wow · controversial · how-to · BTS.
2. **Pick 4–6, each with a different treatment:** straight clip with a new hook · re-voiced recap · text-only scroller (F17) · react to your own clip (F07) ·
   carousel (6–10 slides: slide 1 is the hook, the last slide is the save or share CTA) · story poll · YouTube community post.
3. **Schedule** over 7–10 days, alternating formats; never 2 in a row from the same moment.
4. **Cross-posting:** Reel → Short (clean export, new title, check the music licence). Carousel → LinkedIn document post (good for a CA audience).

## 3. Analytics review
**Collect** (per post): views, reach, 3s hold or skip rate, average watch time / % watched, retention graph drop points, shares (sends), saves, comments, follows, and for long-form, impressions CTR.
**Normalise:** shares per 1k views, saves per 1k views, follows per 1k views.
**Rough benchmarks for a small account** (directional only; compare against your own median):
| Metric | Weak | OK | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3s hold (viewers still watching) | < 50% | 50–70% | > 70% |
| Avg % watched (30–60s reel) | < 30% | 30–50% | > 50% |
| Shares / 1k views | < 3 | 3–10 | > 10 |
| Saves / 1k views | < 5 | 5–15 | > 15 |
| Follows / 1k views | < 1 | 1–5 | > 5 |
**Diagnose:**
- Low 3s hold → hook problem
- Good hold but low average → the middle sags (tighten it, add a re-hook)
- Watched but not shared → not relatable or surprising enough
- Shared but no follows → unclear identity (fix the bio, add a series promise like "har Thursday ₹ angle")
- Saves high → make more of that format and topic
**Pattern-find:** group by format, hook, structure, pace, effect family, grade, language and length. With fewer than 3 posts per group, it's only a hint.
**Experiments:** 2–3 per week, each changing **one** variable, with a hypothesis and a success metric. Output a **Keep / Kill / Try** list.

## 4. Growth playbook (0 → 1k → 10k)
- **0–1k:** 3 posts a week for 12 weeks without breaks; 2 permanent series; reply to every comment; 1 collab a month with a peer creator;
  comment thoughtfully on 5 bigger creators in your niche each day (be a real contributor, not "nice post").
- **1k–10k:** double down on the top 2 formats from the analytics; start YouTube long-form monthly; turn top comments into reply videos;
  build a free resource (a checklist or guide) for "comment KEYWORD" (educational, and compliant with professional rules).
- **Always:** never buy followers or use engagement pods. They hurt distribution signals.

## 5. Monetisation (later; check professional rules first)
Platform programs (when eligible) · brand collaborations with paid-partnership labels (avoid products that conflict with ICAI or employer rules) ·
paid workshops or courses (educational) · affiliate links for books or tools (disclose them).
For employed CAs: check the employment contract and firm policy before any paid creator income.

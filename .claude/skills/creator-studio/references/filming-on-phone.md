# Filming on a Phone (SHOOT)

## 1. Phone settings (Android)
- **Rear camera** for quality (use a mirror, flip screen or the phone screen's reflection to frame yourself); front camera only in strong light.
- **Resolution:** 4K 30fps for talking (gives room to punch in); 1080p/4K **60fps** for anything you'll slow down; 24/25fps for a cinematic b-roll look.
- **Lock exposure and focus:** long-press on your face → AE/AF lock (most Android camera apps). Use Pro mode if available: ISO as low as possible, shutter ≈ 1/(2×fps), and white balance fixed (don't use auto WB).
- Turn off beauty filters and heavy HDR "auto" smoothing. Clean the lens every time.
- The ultra-wide (0.5x) lens distorts faces. Use it for rooms, establishing shots and the phone-on-floor angle only.
- Turn on gridlines; put your eyes on the top-third line and leave a little headroom.
- Use airplane mode while recording; enable Do Not Disturb.

## 2. Light (in order of cost)
1. **Window light:** face the window, or put it at 45°. Avoid having the window behind you.
2. **White wall bounce:** a sheet or chart paper opposite the window to fill shadows.
3. **One LED panel or ring light:** at 45° above eye level, diffused; turn off mixed overhead tubes if they cause colour clash.
4. **Practical lights** in the background (a lamp, fairy lights) add depth and a premium feel.
Golden hour (about the hour after sunrise and before sunset) suits travel and off-duty shots.

## 3. Audio
A clip-on wireless mic is the biggest upgrade. Without one, record close to the phone in a quiet, soft room, or record a VO separately in a cupboard full of clothes.

## 4. Framing & backgrounds
- **Talking head:** mid-shot (chest up) or close-up; you a little off-centre; depth behind you (not flat against a wall).
- **Brand background:** 1–2 objects that signal the niche (books, a calculator, a world map, a plant) plus a brand-colour touch.
- **Vary the set** across videos: desk, standing by a bookshelf, balcony, car (parked), walking outside, café.

## 5. Camera styles (rotate, `libraries.md` §10)
Tripod mid-shot · handheld walk-and-talk · top-down desk (documents, calculator, cards) · phone on the floor (ultra-wide) · over-the-shoulder at a laptop · mirror shot · low hero angle · selfie at arm's length · static wide + punch-ins.

## 6. Shot list format
| # | Beat | Shot (ECU/CU/MS/WS) | Angle | Movement | Lens | Duration to film | In-camera transition | Notes |
- Film each important beat from 2 angles (e.g. MS + CU).
- **B-roll bank (8–15 shots, 3–5s each):** hands typing, calculator, documents being signed (no real client info!), passport, rupee notes or coins,
  a petrol pump display, a phone screen with a news app, a city street, walking feet, coffee, a world map or globe, airport or train, a laptop with a portal.
- **In-camera transitions:** note the out-shot and in-shot (whip, hand cover, match pose, jump out of frame).

## 7. Batching (5–7 hours a week)
- Film 2–3 videos in one session; change your top and background between videos so they don't look the same.
- Group shots by location and light, not by script order.
- Keep an "evergreen b-roll" folder; add 10 new clips every week.

## 8. Professional caution (finance or employed creators)
Never film office premises, laptop screens with work data, client documents, ID cards or a lanyard showing the employer.

# Scripting (SCRIPT)

## Word budgets
| Length | English (~2.5 words/s) | Hinglish (~3 words/s) | Beats |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15s | 35 | 45 | Hook + 1 point + payoff |
| 30s | 70 | 90 | Hook + 2–3 points + CTA |
| 45s | 105 | 135 | Hook + setup + 3 points + CTA |
| 60s | 140 | 180 | Hook + story or 3–4 points + payoff |
| 90s | 210 | 270 | Mini-doc: 3 acts |
Cut 15% after the first draft. Every sentence must either move the story forward or set up the payoff.

## Story structures (rotate; full list in `libraries.md` §3)
- **S01 PAS:** problem (relatable) → agitate (cost) → solve (steps) → CTA
- **S02 Hook-Story-Lesson:** personal moment → twist → takeaway
- **S03 Countdown:** #3 → #2 → #1 (best last), with on-screen checkpoints
- **S04 Open loop:** tease the answer at 0s, reveal at the end, re-tease mid-way
- **S06 Mini doc:** setup (who/what) → conflict (why it matters) → resolution (what it means for you)
- **S07 Q → wrong answers → right answer:** good for myths
- **S09 Circular loop:** the last line flows into the first (makes viewers rewatch)
- **S11 In medias res:** start at the climax, then "3 days earlier…"
- **S14 Choose your path:** "Comment A or B for part 2"

## Explainer formula (for news, tax and finance)
1. **What happened** in 2 facts → 2. **Why** with one analogy → 3. **₹ angle / so what for you** in 2–3 concrete effects →
4. **What to watch or do** (educational) → 5. Signature close.
Good analogies use everyday Indian life: kitchen budget, cricket, EMI, petrol pump, monthly ration, wedding expenses.

## Retention devices (insert 2–3, from `libraries.md` §11)
Re-hook at about 40% ("Lekin asli twist ye hai…"), numbered checkpoints, an unfinished sentence across a cut, b-roll whenever a noun is said, a progress bar, a loop ending.

## Writing style rules
- Write the way the creator speaks: short sentences, contractions, their slang, and their own code-switching.
- Use one idea per video. If you have 2, make 2 videos.
- Use numbers over adjectives ("₹46,000" not "a lot").
- Write **on-screen text separately** from spoken text: on-screen is the keyword summary, not a transcript.
- **CTAs tied to value:** "Save this for ITR season", "Send this to the NRI in your family", "Comment SECTION for part 2". Avoid "like share subscribe".

## Long-form vlog / explainer (8–15 min)
1. Cold open (0:00–0:30) → 2. 3–6 chapters, each with a mini-hook, a turn and a payoff → 3. Re-hooks every 60–90s →
4. One quiet, honest moment in the second half → 5. Resolution plus a teaser for the next video. No long goodbye.
For each chapter include talking points, must-get shots, b-roll, a graphic or map idea, and the music change.
Also give 3 titles (≤ 60 characters), thumbnail text (≤ 4 words), and chapter timestamps for the description.

## Teleprompter tip
Record in short sections of 1–2 lines each and look at the lens. Use a teleprompter app or the Edits teleprompter (if your version has one).
Re-record any line where your energy drops. Jump cuts are fine and expected.

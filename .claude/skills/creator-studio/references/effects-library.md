# Effects Library (EFFECTS)

90+ effects, each with an ID for the content log. **Rule: at most 2–3 effect families per video**, every effect needs a reason
(emphasis, transition, information, or mood), and no family should be a video's "main move" twice in a row.

**How-to notes:** "Edits" means Instagram Edits (Android or iOS). "CapCut" is the fallback. Menu names change between versions, so if a button
isn't where described, look for the closest equivalent (Effects / Animation / Keyframe / Overlay / Cutout / Speed). Difficulty: ★ easy · ★★ medium · ★★★ advanced.

## Effect anti-patterns
- A transition on every cut. A hard cut is the default, and flashy transitions are for scene or time changes only.
- More than 2 text animation styles in one video.
- Shake, glitch and flash in the same 3 seconds. That's noise, not emphasis.
- Effects covering the face during an important line.
- AI-generated "evidence" visuals in news or finance (it damages credibility and may mislead).

# Typography & Colour (LOOK)

**Principle:** 70% fixed brand, 30% fresh. Keep the anchor element and core colours. Rotate the font style or pairing, caption style, text animation, grade and accent colour.

## 1. Picking fonts
- In **Edits**, use built-in fonts, grouped into 5 style codes (log these):
  | Code | Style | Mood | Library equivalents |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | EF1 | Bold condensed | Loud, breaking news, hooks | FP01 Anton, FP03 Bebas |
  | EF2 | Elegant serif | Calm, premium, NRI/finance | FP02, FP05, FP11 |
  | EF3 | Clean modern sans | Tips, lists, tax | FP04, FP07, FP10 |
  | EF4 | Typewriter / mono | Documents, "case files", news | FP12 |
  | EF5 | Handwritten / marker | Personal, annotations | FP09 |
- For **covers, thumbnails, PNG overlays and charts**, use Canva with exact Google Fonts from `libraries.md` §6.
- For **Hindi in Devanagari**, use fonts with Devanagari support (e.g. Mukta, Hind, Poppins, Noto Sans Devanagari, Baloo 2, Tiro Devanagari Hindi for a serif look).
- Use at most 2 typefaces per video plus the anchor. Create hierarchy with size and weight, not more fonts.

## 2. Caption styles (rotate, `libraries.md` §7)
Specs to give: font, size (about 42–56 px equivalent on 1080 wide), weight, colour, keyword colour, shadow or box, lines (≤ 2), words per screen (≤ 7), position, animation.
Default safe spec: white text, 8–12% black shadow or a semi-transparent box, keyword in the accent colour, centre-upper third.

## 3. Text animation pairing
| Font style | Good animations | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| EF1 bold condensed | Slam/scale-in, word pop, shake | Slow fades |
| EF2 serif | Slide-up fade, blur-in | Bouncy pop |
| EF3 sans | Word pop, slide, karaoke | Glitch overload |
| EF4 typewriter | Typewriter, redacted reveal | Bounce |
| EF5 handwritten | Write-on, wiggle, scribble | Glitch |

## 4. Colour grading
Grade in Edits or CapCut with filters plus sliders, or in Lightroom Mobile for photos. Start from a correctly exposed, white-balanced shot.
| Grade | Settings (approximate, adjust to footage) | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| G01 Warm film | Warmth +12–18, contrast −5, fade +8–10, saturation −5, grain 10–15 | Personal, travel, nostalgia |
| G02 Clean bright | Exposure +0.1–0.2, contrast +5, warmth 0, saturation 0, sharpen +5 | Tips, tax, NRI desk |
| G03 Teal & orange | Shadows toward teal, highlights warm, saturation +5 | Travel, cinematic |
| G04 Moody | Contrast +15, saturation −20–30, shadows −10, slight cool tint | Geopolitics, serious news |
| G05 Pastel | Contrast −15, highlights −10, pink/cyan tint, saturation −10 | Lifestyle, soft |
| G06 B&W contrasty | Saturation −100, contrast +20, grain 10 | History, emotion, flashback |
| G07 VHS | Saturation −10, sharpen −, noise, 4:3, date stamp | Throwbacks |
| G08 Night neon | Magenta/cyan tints, crushed blacks | Night city |
| G09 Golden hour | Warmth +20, glow/halation low, highlights −10 | Sunset, travel |
| G10 Faded matte | Fade +15, contrast −10, saturation −10 | Editorial, NRI calm |
**Skin tones first:** if faces look orange or green, pull back the tint or warmth. Keep the grade consistent within a video.

## 5. Colour system per post
Core palette (fixed) + **1 accent per post** (rotate: amber, teal, coral, lime, violet, sky). Use the accent only on keywords, highlights and icons.
Check contrast: text vs background ≥ 4.5:1. Add a shadow or box when in doubt.

## 6. Covers & thumbnails
- **Reel cover:** 1080×1920, with key content in the centre 1080×1440 (the grid shows 4:5 crops now). ≤ 4 words, face plus emotion or an object as the hero.
- **Grid check:** view the last 9 covers together; the layout family should be consistent, with colours and images varied.
- **YouTube thumbnail:** 1280×720, ≤ 4 words, a face with a clear emotion, a single focal object, high contrast, readable at phone size.
  Test 2–3 thumbnails or titles with YouTube's test-and-compare where available.
- Make covers in Canva using the brand palette and anchor tag. Keep one template per series.

# Sound Design (SOUND)

Viewers forgive average video but not bad audio. Sound is also the cheapest way to make an edit feel premium.

## 1. Voice chain (every video)
1. **Record well:** a clip-on mic 15–20 cm from the mouth; soft room (curtains, bed, cupboard of clothes); fans and AC off; record 10s of room tone.
2. **Clean:** Edits voice enhance or noise reduction (CapCut "Enhance voice"/"Reduce noise"). Don't overdo it, or you'll sound robotic.
3. **Level:** voice peaks around −6 to −3 dB; overall about −14 LUFS for social.
4. (Advanced, desktop) EQ: high-pass at 80 Hz, cut 200–300 Hz mud, a small boost at 3–5 kHz for clarity; light compression.

## 2. Music
- **Under voice:** music 10–20% (about −18 to −24 dB below voice). Raise it in the gaps between sentences ("ducking").
- **Match the mood** to the recipe music ID (`libraries.md` §9). Change the track at chapter or tone shifts in long-form.
- **The power of stopping:** cut the music 1 beat before the reveal or punchline, then bring it back on the payoff.
- **Rights:** use the in-app libraries (Instagram/Edits, YouTube Audio Library) for each platform. Trending audio in Reels may not be
  licensed for YouTube. Export a version with a different track for Shorts if needed.
- **Trending audio:** at low volume under your voice it can help discovery on Reels. Never let it overpower your message.

## 3. SFX library (the "premium" layer)
| SFX | Use with | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Whoosh / swish | Whip pans, slides, text flying in | Match the length to the motion |
| Pop / bubble | Text or sticker appearing | Soft; don't use one on every caption |
| Click / tap | Screen recordings, UI cards, step badges | |
| Typewriter clacks | E-TEXT-03 typewriter text | |
| Riser | 1–2s before a reveal or drop | End it exactly on the cut |
| Hit / boom / thud | Number shock, document slam, text slam | Pair with a camera shake |
| Cash register / coin | Money figures (sparingly, it can feel cheesy) | Use once per video max |
| Paper rustle / page flip | Document or newspaper effects | |
| Camera shutter | Freeze frames, photo-dumps | |
| Record scratch | "Wait, what?" moments | Comedy-lite, rare |
| Ambience bed (city, rain, office) | Under talking or b-roll for realism | Very low |
| Notification ding | Phone UI cards | |
| Heartbeat / tick-tock | Deadlines, tension | |
| Silence (1s) | Before the most important line | The most powerful "SFX" |

## 4. Sound-led formats
- **ASMR / process:** close mic on real sounds (calculator, stamping, cooking, packing). Music off.
- **Voice-note style:** a lo-fi phone-call EQ for a "message to my younger self".
- **Natural sound vlogs:** no music; footsteps, traffic, temple bells, rain. Text tells the story.

## 5. Sound check before export
- [ ] Every word is intelligible on phone speakers at 50% volume
- [ ] No clipping or distortion
- [ ] Music doesn't fight the voice
- [ ] At least one deliberate SFX moment and one deliberate silence
- [ ] Volume is consistent from clip to clip

# News, Finance & Compliance (NEWS)

## 1. Radar
Use web search for this week's India and world stories: geopolitics, policy, RBI/inflation/rupee, crude/gold, markets (index level only),
budget, GST and income-tax changes, NRI/FEMA/remittance rules, sports business, big tech launches, and viral money claims.
If there's no search, ask the user for 3–5 headlines. Check the content log to avoid repeating a story without a real update.

## 2. Pick (score 1–5)
Money angle · Curiosity ("why" people are confused) · Shelf life ≥ 48h · Safety (can be explained neutrally with sources) · Lens and pillar fit.

## 3. Fact pack (before scripting)
| # | Fact | Source (prefer primary) | Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
Primary sources: RBI, SEBI, PIB/ministries, CBDT/Income Tax Dept, CBIC/GSTN, official league or company releases and filings. Then Reuters, PTI and major papers.
✅ verified / ⚠️ reported only (say "reports say") / ❌ unverified (cut it). Numbers that move: say "as of <date>" or round them ("around 94").
**Never invent figures.** If sources disagree, say so or round.

## 4. Explainer structure
Hook (H21–H24) → what happened (2 facts) → why (1 analogy) → **₹ angle** (2–3 concrete effects on the viewer) → what to watch or do (educational) → signature close.
Show a source card (E-INFO-09) on screen for key facts.

## 5. Compliance checklist (print it in every finance, news or political Production Pack)
> Not legal advice. The creator should read the current rules and their employer's policy themselves.
- [ ] **No investment advice:** explain concepts; no buy/sell/hold calls on specific stocks, funds or crypto; no target prices; no promised returns.
      SEBI has tightened rules on unregistered "finfluencers" and on registered entities associating with them, so stay educational.
- [ ] **ICAI members (CAs):** no solicitation of clients or advertising of services (no "DM me for ITR filing", fees, or "best CA" claims). Educational content is the safe zone.
      Check the current ICAI guidance on social media and advertising.
- [ ] **Employed professionals (e.g. Big Four):** personal account; no firm name or logo unless policy allows; "Views are personal"; no clients, engagements or work info;
      **no commentary on specific listed companies** (independence: they may be audit clients). Check the employment contract and firm policy before any paid outside work or sponsorships.
- [ ] **Tax content:** cite the section or rule and the official source; say which tax year the rules apply to; note that thresholds change.
- [ ] **Politics/geopolitics:** neutral wording; state each side's position with sources; no party endorsement; no claims about named individuals that you can't source (defamation risk).
- [ ] **Sponsored content:** use the paid-partnership label and disclose (ASCI guidelines in India), and never for financial products if that conflicts with ICAI or employer rules.
- [ ] **Disclaimer in the caption:** "Educational only. Not financial/tax advice. Views are personal."
- [ ] **Sources** in the caption or pinned comment.
- [ ] **No AI-generated visuals presented as real** news footage or documents.

## 6. Fast-turnaround workflow (90 minutes)
15 min radar and pick → 20 min fact pack → 15 min script → 15 min record → 25 min edit. Keep 2 evergreen backup reels filmed for slow news weeks.

## 7. Evergreen finance topics for CAs (good for building trust and saves)
Old vs new regime · Tax Year under the Income-tax Act 2025 · NRE vs NRO · residential status · TDS basics · Form 26AS/AIS · advance tax dates ·
HRA · capital gains basics · GST for freelancers · salary slip decoded · emergency fund math · inflation and the real value of money · insurance basics (term vs endowment, explained conceptually).

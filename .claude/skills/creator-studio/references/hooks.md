# Hooks (HOOK)

## Anatomy: 4 layers in the first 1–3 seconds
| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Visual** | Motion in frame 1 (you mid-gesture, an object moving, a zoom). A face or hands beats scenery. Show the payoff if you can |
| **Text** | ≤ 7 words, readable in 1s, centre-upper third, in the bold font of the recipe |
| **Spoken** | ≤ 12 words. Start mid-thought. No greeting |
| **Sound** | An SFX hit, music downbeat or natural sound that signals something is happening |
It must work **with the sound off** (visual + text) and **with it on** (spoken + sound).

## Process
1. Read the topic, the takeaway and the last 5 hook IDs in the log.
2. Draft 6 hooks from different archetypes (`libraries.md` §2), score each on clarity, tension and fit, recommend 1 plus a backup.
3. Check that the hook's promise is paid off in the video. Clickbait loses viewers by second 5.

## Hinglish / English hook bank (adapt, don't copy word for word)
**Contrarian:** "Salary account mein paisa rakhna sabse mehenga galti hai." · "Stop filing ITR in July."
**Curiosity gap:** "NRI ke liye ek rule jo koi CA nahi batata." · "Ye ek number decide karta hai aapka EMI."
**Number shock:** "₹1.8 lakh crore. Ek din mein." · "93.9. Ye number aapki foreign trip sasti kar raha hai."
**Result-first:** (show the saved-tax screenshot) "₹46,000 bachaye. Bas ek form se."
**Direct callout:** "Agar aap NRI ho aur India mein ghar hai, ye sunlo." · "First salary aayi hai? Ruko."
**Question:** "Aapke ₹1 lakh ka asli value 10 saal baad kitna hoga?"
**Stakes/deadline:** "31 December ke baad ye option band."
**Mistake confession:** "Mera pehla ITR galat tha. Ye galti mat karna."
**Everyone's wrong:** "Sab keh rahe hain petrol sasta hoga. Asli kahani alag hai."
**POV:** "POV: aapka CA dost aapko ₹ angle samjha raha hai."
**Comparison tease:** "₹500 vs ₹5,000 ka insurance, fark dekho."
**Map open:** (zoomed map) "Ye chhoti si jagah duniya ka 20% tel control karti hai."
**Text-only bold claim:** black frame, big type: "ASSESSMENT YEAR IS DEAD."
**Wrong-then-right:** do the common wrong thing first, then a hard stop: "Galat."
**Overheard:** "Mere colleague ne kaha, 'NRE account mein tax lagta hai.' Nahi lagta."

## Visual hook moves (pair with any spoken hook)
- **Throw or slide an object into frame** (calculator, passport, newspaper)
- **Snap zoom** from wide to a face on the first word (E-ZOOM-01)
- **Text behind subject** reveal on frame 1 (E-VFX-02)
- **Document slam:** a highlighted headline zooms in with a camera shake (E-NEWS-01)
- **Mid-walk start:** you're already walking toward the camera, talking
- **Unusual angle:** phone on the floor, top-down or behind an object (camera styles CS06/CS10)
- **Before/after split:** frame 1 is already split screen

## Banned openers
"Hey guys welcome back" · "In today's video" · "Wait till the end" · "Don't skip" · logo intros longer than 1s · static scenery for 3s · asking people to follow before giving any value.

## Long-form cold open (first 30s)
Best moment teaser (5–8s) → the stakes or promise line → why trust me (1 line) → the title card with the anchor element → straight into chapter 1.

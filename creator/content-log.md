## Repeat rules (used by all skills)
- **Format:** not the same format twice in a row; at most 2 times in any 7 posts.
- **Hook archetype:** not repeated within the last 5 posts.
- **Story structure:** not repeated within the last 3 posts.
- **Font pairing & grade:** not repeated within the last 4 posts (brand anchor font excluded).
- **Transition style & music mood:** not repeated within the last 3 posts.
- **Language:** across any 10 posts, at least 2 in English-only and 2 in Hindi-only.
- **Font pairing** may be logged as an Edits style code (EF1–EF5); don't use the same EF code twice in a row.
- **Exception:** a deliberate series (same look on purpose) is allowed when it's marked `SERIES:<name>` in Notes.

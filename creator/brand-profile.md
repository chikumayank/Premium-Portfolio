### Positioning (recommended)
> **"Har headline ka paisa wala angle."** I explain what the news, the world and life
> mean for your money, in simple Hinglish, the way a CA would.

**Why this angle:** you like geopolitics, politics, sports, tech, travel, fitness, finance, accounts and NRI topics.
Posting all of them separately would confuse both the algorithm and your audience ("what is this account?").
A **money lens** connects them. A war becomes "why petrol and gold moved", a cricket auction becomes "how the
IPL franchise math works", and a trip becomes "my Goa trip, CA-style breakdown". One identity covers every topic.

Alternatives, if this one doesn't feel right:
- "The NRI's CA friend": narrower, with an engaged and high-value audience, and easier to grow fast
- "Desi world explainer": a geopolitics and news explainer with finance as just one pillar. This is broader, and political reach is limited (see below).

## Audience
- Primary viewer: 22–40, Indian urban salaried people and young professionals, CA/commerce students, and NRIs (Gulf, US, UK, Canada, Australia)
- What they want: to **understand** what's going on and **what to do about it** with their money, in under a minute
- What they're tired of seeing: shouting "finfluencers", stock-tip clickbait, 10-minute videos that could be 45 seconds, and the same yellow captions on everything

## Voice
- 3 adjectives: **clear, calm-confident, desi-witty** (smart, not comedy)
- Keep: "Simple bhasha mein…", "Paisa wala angle ye hai…", explaining with numbers and analogies
- Avoid: "Guys", "Wait till the end", "Ye video skip mat karna", fear-mongering, and stock tips like "buy this stock"
- Energy on camera: 6–7/10 (alert, never shouting)

## Never us
- Comedy or skits as a main format
- Party-political cheerleading. Explain policies, never campaign for a party
- Stock or crypto buy/sell tips, guaranteed returns, "get rich" claims
- Ragebait thumbnails, fake "BREAKING" sirens
- The same font and caption style in two posts back-to-back

## Professional guardrails (CA + Big Four employee)
> Not legal advice. Read your employer's social media / outside-activities policy and the latest ICAI guidance yourself, once, before relaunching.
- **Personal account only.** No firm name, logo, office photos, ID cards, or "Big 4 CA" in the bio or videos unless the firm's policy allows it. Add "Views are personal" to the bio.
- **Confidentiality:** never mention clients, deals, audits, engagements or anything learnt at work, even anonymised "a client of mine…" stories.
- **Independence:** no comments, positive or negative, on specific listed companies or their stocks. Your firm may audit them. Stay at the level of economy, policy, tax law and personal finance concepts.
- **No soliciting:** no "DM me to file your ITR", no fees or service menus, no "best CA" claims. Educational content is the safe zone.
- **Freelancing:** check whether your employment contract and your ICAI certificate-of-practice status allow paid outside work. Keep freelancing completely separate from this account.
- **Disclaimers:** finance reels get "Educational only, not financial or tax advice. Views are personal." in the caption.

# Instagram Edits Playbook (Android, beginner → confident)

> Edits is Instagram's free editing app, so it's all you need for now. Feature names and menus change between
> versions. If a button isn't where this guide says, look for the closest equivalent or search the app's help.

## Setup (one time, 15 min)
1. Install **Edits** from the Play Store and log in with your Instagram account.
2. Phone camera: **1080p or 4K at 30fps** for talking, **60fps** for b-roll you want to slow down. Clean the lens every time.
3. Turn **gridlines** on in your camera to help framing (eyes on the top third line).
4. Make a Canva folder called "Reel kit" containing: the **₹ ANGLE tag** (transparent PNG), 3 cover templates, and
   1 end-card. Save them to your phone's gallery so Edits can import them as overlays.

## The 10 moves to learn (one or two per week)
| # | Move | What it's for | Where to look in Edits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trim + split clips | Remove pauses and "umm". This alone makes reels feel 2× faster | Select clip → Split / drag edges |
| 2 | Auto captions | Captions in Hindi/English/Hinglish | Captions / Text → auto-captions, then fix spelling by hand |
| 3 | Caption style changes | Fight the "same yellow captions" problem | Tap captions → style/font/colour/animation |
| 4 | Text overlays + timing | Key numbers on screen, ₹ ANGLE moments | Text → add → drag on timeline |
| 5 | Image/sticker overlay | Your ₹ ANGLE PNG, charts, screenshots | Overlay / add media as layer |
| 6 | Green screen | Explain a headline, chart or map behind you | Green screen / cutout effect |
| 7 | Zoom punch-ins | Emphasis on key words without a second camera | Keyframes on scale (100% → 115%) or zoom effect |
| 8 | Voice enhance / noise reduction | Clean audio. Viewers forgive video quality, not audio | Audio → voice enhance / noise reduction |
| 9 | Sound effects + music volume | Whoosh, pop and click accents; music under voice | Audio → sound effects; lower music volume to ~10–20% under speech |
| 10 | Transitions (sparingly) | One deliberate transition, not one on every cut | Tap between clips → transition |

## Font strategy in Edits
Edits uses its own built-in font set, so you can't load any Google Font. Rotate between these **5 font families of style**, labelled so the
content log can track them:
| Code | Style to pick in Edits | Use for | Library equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| EF1 | Bold condensed (tall, heavy) | Breaking-style news hooks | FP01 / FP03 |
| EF2 | Elegant serif | Calm explainers, NRI Desk | FP02 / FP05 / FP11 |
| EF3 | Clean modern sans | Tax/accounts tips, lists | FP04 / FP07 |
| EF4 | Typewriter / mono | Documents, "case file" geopolitics | FP12 |
| EF5 | Handwritten / marker | Off-duty, personal, annotations | FP09 |
For **covers and thumbnails**, use Canva with the exact library fonts (e.g. Anton, Playfair Display, Fraunces).
Canva gives you precise fonts there.

## Colour (grades) in Edits
Use the app's filters plus adjustment sliders. Save your 3 favourite combinations as presets mentally or with screenshots:
- **G02 clean bright:** brightness +5, contrast +5, saturation 0, warmth 0. For tax tips and NRI Desk.
- **G04 moody:** contrast +15, saturation −25, shadows −10, slight cool tint. For geopolitics and serious news.
- **G01 warm film:** warmth +15, contrast −5, fade +10, grain/texture if available. For off-duty and travel.

## Safe zone
Keep text away from the **bottom 20%** (caption and username) and the **right 15%** (like/comment buttons). Centre-upper third is safest.

## Export & post
- Export at the highest quality. Post to Instagram directly from Edits if you like.
- Download a copy and upload the same file to **YouTube Shorts**, with a new title and the first line of the caption as a hook.
- Try **trial reels** (shown to non-followers first) when testing a new format or hook.
